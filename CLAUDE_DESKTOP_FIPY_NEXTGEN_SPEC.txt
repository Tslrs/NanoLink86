1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & GOAL
================================================================================
The objective is to design a high-end, open-source, ultra-compact (55 x 20 mm)
multi-radio IoT development board. It modernizes the legacy Pycom FiPy by:
1. Eliminating physical plastic nano-SIM slots in favor of an on-board soldered eSIM.
2. Replacing delicate u.FL connectors with rugged, screw-on SMA connectors.
3. Upgrading wireless modems to current generation standards (SX1262, Wi-Fi 6, Zigbee 3.0).
4. Solving all RF cross-talk and power-rail interference through advanced 4-layer
   PCB layout, RF shielding cans, and SAW bandpass filtering.

================================================================================
2. DETAILED BILL OF MATERIALS (BOM) & COMPONENT SELECTION
================================================================================

A. Main Compute Unit (Host MCU):
   - Primary: Raspberry Pi RP2350 (Dual ARM Cortex-M33 / Hazard3 RISC-V @ 150MHz,
     520KB SRAM, hardware security, TrustZone, 8MB External QSPI Flash).
   - Alternative Option: Espressif ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (Dual Xtensa LX7, 8MB PSRAM).

B. Cellular & Global Cloud Connectivity (4G LTE-M / NB-IoT / 2G):
   - Module: Quectel BG95-M3 or SIMCom SIM7080G (LGA module).
   - Features: Global multi-band LTE Cat M1 / Cat NB2 / EGPRS. Ultra-low power sleep.
   - Embedded SIM (eSIM): STMicroelectronics ST4SIM-200M (Industrial MFF2 package,
     5x6mm DFN-8). Pre-provisioned for global roaming over cellular networks.

C. Long-Range Sub-GHz Radio (LoRa):
   - Transceiver: Semtech SX1262 (QFN-24).
   - Frequency: 868 MHz (Europe) / 915 MHz (US).
   - RF Performance: Up to +22 dBm output power, -148 dBm sensitivity.
   - RF Protection: On-board SAW Filter (EPCOS/TDK B39871B3725U410 or similar 868MHz bandpass).
   - Antenna Connector: Edge-mount SMA female connector (rugged, screw-on).

D. 2.4 GHz Multi-Protocol Co-Processor (Wi-Fi 6 + BLE 5 + Zigbee 3.0 + Thread):
   - RF SoC: Espressif ESP32-C6FH4 (QFN-32, 4MB embedded flash).
   - Protocols: 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6 @ 2.4GHz), Bluetooth 5 (LE) + Mesh, Zigbee 3.0,
     Matter, and OpenThread.
   - Interface to RP2350 Host: High-speed SPI + UART + GPIO control lines.
   - Antenna: Ceramic chip antenna (Johanson 2450AT18D0100) with matching network.

E. Power Management (PMIC) & Battery Subsystem:
   - Buck Converter (DC-DC): Texas Instruments TPS62088 (3A continuous, 4A peak,
     2.4MHz step-down). Essential for suppressing voltage drops during LTE 2A transmit bursts.
   - Low-Noise LDO (for sensitive Analog/RF blocks): Microchip MIC5365 (3.3V, 150mA, ultra-low ripple).
   - LiPo Charger & Power Path: TI BQ24075 (USB / Battery power path management with dynamic
     system power management and thermal regulation).
   - USB Interface: USB Type-C connector with ESD protection (USBLC6-2SC6) and CC pull-downs.

3. ZERO-INTERFERENCE RF DESIGN & SHIELDING STRATEGY
================================================================================
Operating 4G (800-900MHz), LoRa (868MHz), and 2.4GHz (Wi-Fi/BLE/Zigbee) on a
tiny 55x20mm PCB causes massive cross-talk unless these exact rules are enforced:

1. 4-Layer PCB Stackup:
   - Layer 1 (Top Layer): Controlled impedance RF traces (50 Ohm coplanar waveguide),
     critical high-speed traces, and active IC components.
   - Layer 2 (Ground Plane): SOLID, UNBROKEN COPPER GROUND PLANE (GND). Zero traces
     crossing underneath RF transmission lines! Acts as a shield barrier.
   - Layer 3 (Power Plane): 3.3V System, 3.8V Cellular power rails, and battery voltage.
   - Layer 4 (Bottom Layer): Digital control signals (SPI, I2C, UART, GPIOs) and passive passives.

2. Physical RF Shielding Cans (Metal Shields):
   - Place two standard soldered metal shielding cans (SMD shielding frames + clips):
     * Can #1: Enclosing the Quectel LTE modem and its immediate RF front-end.
     * Can #2: Enclosing the Semtech SX1262 LoRa transceiver and crystal oscillator.
   - These prevent high-power cellular harmonic radiation from coupling into the sensitive
     LoRa receiver front-end.

3. Out-of-Band Filtering:
   - The LoRa RF output MUST pass through an 868 MHz SAW bandpass filter before reaching
     the antenna. This attenuates LTE Band 20 (791-862 MHz) signals by >40 dB, preventing
     cellular transmissions from blinding the LoRa receiver.

4. Spatial Antenna Separation:
   - Cellular antenna connection: Positioned at Board Edge A.
   - LoRa SMA antenna connection: Positioned at Board Edge B (opposite side).
   - 2.4 GHz ceramic antenna: Positioned in an isolated corner with continuous keep-out
     areas on all copper layers directly underneath the antenna radiator.

5. Via Stitching / Via Fencing:
   - Place ground stitching vias spaced lambda/20 (approx. 1.0 to 1.5 mm apart) along the
     entire length of all 50 Ohm RF coplanar waveguides to confine electromagnetic fields.

6. Coexistence Signaling (Hardware Coex):
   - Dedicated hardware lines between the Cellular modem and ESP32-C6 (TX_ACTIVE, STATUS,
     PRIORITY) to enable Time-Division Multiplexing (TDM) during heavy traffic.
